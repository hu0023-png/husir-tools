📣 Poll & Vote Counter

Quick classroom or meeting polls · tap to vote · live bars · runs 100% in your browser

📘 About this tool

Set up a question with two or more options and tap the screen to cast votes in real time. Each option shows a running total and a live progress bar, and the current winner is highlighted automatically — perfect for a quick classroom show-of-hands replacement, picking a team activity, or settling a small-group preference without anyone raising their voice. A single device passes around the room, or students vote on their own phones by opening the page.

Every vote can be taken back with the small minus button if someone changes their mind, and the poll stays saved in your browser, so your results survive a refresh. Copy the results as a clean text summary to paste into a chat, email, or lesson plan. Everything runs locally on your device — no accounts, no uploads, fully offline.

📅 How many options can I have?
Two to twelve options per poll. Keep options short so the vote buttons stay readable on a phone.

🔁 What happens if I refresh the page?
Your poll and its votes are restored from this browser’s local storage. “New poll” clears them.

🏆 How is the winner decided?
Whichever option has the most votes. If two or more tie for first, the page says so instead of picking one arbitrarily.

📤 Can one person vote more than once?
This is a quick tally tool, not a secure ballot. There is no voter authentication — it trusts the people in the room, which is exactly what classroom polls need.

🔒 Is anything uploaded?
Never. Votes are counted locally on your device with zero network calls.

📐 Quick reference
Total votes = Σ option votes  ·  Share % = option votes ÷ total × 100
Bar width = share %  ·  Winner = max votes (ties reported as a tie)
