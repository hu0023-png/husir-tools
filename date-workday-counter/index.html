📆 Date & Workday Counter

Total days · Workdays · US federal holidays

📘 About this tool

Count exactly what separates two dates: total days, weekends, federal holidays observed, and real working days (Monday–Friday minus holidays). Pick a start and end date and the tool does the calendar math — handy for deadlines, project timelines, payroll periods, and planning time off.

Federal holidays follow the official US rules (third Monday of January, fourth Thursday of November, and so on), and when a holiday lands on a weekend the observed weekday is used automatically. Everything is calculated locally in your browser.

📆 Which holidays are included?
The 11 standard federal holidays: New Year's Day, MLK Jr. Day, Washington's Birthday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas — using the observed date.

🧮 Are the dates inclusive?
Yes — counting from Jan 1 to Jan 3 gives 3 days total. If you prefer exclusive counting, subtract 1 from the result.

🏢 Do all employers follow federal holidays?
No — private employers choose their own holiday calendar. This matches the standard federal schedule, which is also the most common corporate one.

🔒 Is any date sent anywhere?
No — all calculations happen on your device, and the page works offline.

📐 Key formulas
Total days (inclusive) = (end − start) ÷ 86,400,000 + 1
Workdays = count of Mon–Fri − observed federal holidays in range
Weekend days = total days − count of Mon–Fri
