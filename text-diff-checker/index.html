🔍 Text Diff Checker

Compare two versions · see added & removed lines · runs 100% in your browser

📘 About this tool

Need to find what changed between two drafts, two config files, or two versions of an essay? Paste the original on the left and the edited version on the right. The checker compares them line by line and highlights exactly which lines were removed (red), added (green), and kept the same.

Diffing is useful for editors reviewing a rewrite, students checking a revised paragraph, developers sanity-checking a config change, or anyone who received a “new version” of a document and wants to know what actually moved. You can ignore case or whitespace differences when comparing, while the result still shows your original lines. A summary counts unchanged, added, and removed lines, and one click copies a plain-text change list to paste into email or a changelog.

Everything runs locally on your device — your text is never uploaded, no account is needed, and the page works fully offline.

📝 What counts as a change?
Comparison is line-based: identical lines are unchanged, and anything added or removed shows as red or green. Character-level detail inside a line is not highlighted.

🔁 How do the options work?
“Ignore case” treats “Hello” and “hello” as the same for matching. “Ignore whitespace” ignores leading/trailing spaces and indentation when matching lines.

🔒 Is my text sent anywhere?
Never. Both sides are compared entirely in your browser with zero network requests.

🧩 Can it diff code?
Yes — it works on any plain text, including JSON, CSS, or logs. For very large files, keep each side under about 1,500 lines for instant results.

📐 How it works
The tool finds the longest common sequence of lines between the two versions, then walks the result: lines only in the right text are added (+), lines only in the left are removed (−), and lines in both are unchanged.
