Grid Image Tools v6

Grid Split · Panorama Stitch · Template Collage

📘 About this tool

Grid Image Tools slices one photo into an even grid for multi-post social layouts, stitches horizontal or vertical strips back into a single panorama, and assembles template collages. Pick the mode, upload an image, set rows and columns or strips, then download the finished pieces as PNG.

Processing happens on your device: full-resolution tiles, no upload, no watermark, no account. It is useful for Instagram grids, product galleries, printable cutouts, and photo walls.

🖼️ How many pieces will I get?
The number of tiles equals your rows × columns (a 3×2 grid gives 6 tiles), and the preview shows every piece before download.

📸 Does splitting reduce quality?
No — each tile is exported from the original image at full resolution.

🔗 How do I post a seamless social grid?
Upload the tiles in reading order (left to right, top to bottom) and keep the posts in the same grid positions.

🧩 What is stitch for?
Stitching joins strips of a panorama back into one image — upload them in the correct order.

🔒 Is my image uploaded?
No — all processing runs locally.

📐 Quick formulas
Tiles = rows × columns
Export: one PNG per tile, full resolution
Order always runs left → right, top → bottom
