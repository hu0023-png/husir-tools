🌐 Timezone Converter

US & world zones · DST-aware · Meeting planner

📘 About this tool

Schedule across US and world time zones without the mental math. Pick a source zone and time (or use now), then see the instant converted to Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, Alaska, Hawaii, London, Berlin, Beijing, Tokyo, and other zones — with the meeting planner suggesting slots where every participant is inside working hours.

Time zone math runs locally in your browser using the built-in time zone database, so it respects daylight-saving rules without downloading anything. No meetings, dates, or locations are ever sent to a server.

🇺🇸 Which US time zones are covered?
Eastern (ET), Central (CT), Mountain (MT), Pacific (PT), Alaska, and Hawaii — plus UTC and the world's most common business zones.

🌞 Does it handle daylight saving time?
Yes — the converter uses your browser's time zone database, so spring-forward and fall-back dates are applied automatically for zones that observe DST.

🤝 How does the meeting planner work?
It scans the next 7 days in 30-minute steps and highlights times when every selected participant is within a 9:00–17:00 working window in their own zone.

🔒 Is my location shared?
No — everything is computed on your device, and the page works offline once open.

📐 How it works
One instant in time is displayed in every zone — the tool converts UTC to each local wall clock
Difference between zones = (offset of zone A) − (offset of zone B) at that moment
DST changes the offsets on the second Sunday of March and the first Sunday of November (US)
