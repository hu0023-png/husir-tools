🎯 Savings Goal Calculator

How much to save each month · plus a realistic timeline at any amount

📘 About this tool

Whether it is a house down payment, an emergency fund, a wedding, a vacation, or a new car, every savings goal comes down to the same question: how much do I put aside each month to get there on time? This calculator works backwards from the goal: enter what you want, how much you already have, and when you need it, and it tells you the exact monthly deposit — including the interest your money can earn along the way.

It assumes monthly deposits that earn interest every month. For short horizons (under 3–5 years) a high-yield savings account or CD is the normal home for goal money because the balance is safe and liquid; for longer horizons many savers use a brokerage account, which can grow faster but moves up and down. If you already save a fixed amount each month, the tool can also flip the question and show how long your goal will take at that pace.

Everything runs locally in your browser — no account, no login, nothing uploaded. Results are estimates, not promises: rates change, life changes, and markets never grow in a straight line. Re-run it whenever your timeline or income changes.

🏦 What rate should I use?
High-yield savings accounts (HYSA) have paid roughly 3.5–4.5% APY in recent years, while CDs and Treasury bills are similar for fixed terms. For goals more than 5 years away, 6–7% is a common assumption for a diversified stock portfolio. Try a range — a lower rate is the safer plan.

🚨 Why does everyone say start an emergency fund first?
A 3–6 month expense cushion stops surprises (car repair, medical bill, job loss) from forcing you to sell investments or carry credit-card debt. Most advisors suggest building it in a savings account before aggressive goal-saving.

🎢 What if my goal is more than 10 years away?
Long horizons can usually tolerate more growth-focused investing. Still, this calculator assumes one steady return — real returns vary year to year, so check the result at both a conservative and an average rate.

💵 Should I round my monthly deposit up?
Yes. Rounding up a few dollars a month builds a small buffer against rate changes and late starts, and many banks let you automate the transfer so the saving happens before you can spend it.

📈 What if I can only save less than the target?
The compare box shows the honest answer: how many extra months or years the slower pace costs you. Sometimes the best fix is a longer timeline or a slightly smaller goal.

⚠️ Is this financial advice?
No. It is a planning estimate. Taxes, fees, and inflation matter for real-world results — check with a fee-only advisor before making large decisions.

📐 Quick formulas
Monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 12
Future value: balance = current savings × (1 + monthly rate)months + deposit × ((1 + monthly rate)months − 1) ÷ monthly rate
Required monthly deposit = (goal − current savings × (1 + monthly rate)months) × monthly rate ÷ ((1 + monthly rate)months − 1)
Interest earned = goal − current savings − total deposits
