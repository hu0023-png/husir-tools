📘 About this tool

A tiny monthly ledger for tracking money in and out without a full budgeting app. Add an amount with a short note, and the page keeps a running balance for the current month plus a summary — enough for freelancers, students, and anyone who just wants the bottom line.

Entries are stored locally in your browser, so your finances never leave the device. No accounts, no bank connections, and no ads.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Estimates for planning only — not tax or financial advice. Rates and rules change, so verify with your state or a qualified professional before filing or making big decisions.

📅 Which month does it track?
The balance and summary cover the current calendar month; the view naturally starts fresh each month.

🔢 How do I enter amounts?
Type numbers with a decimal (for example 12.50) together with a note so you can remember what each entry was for.

🔒 Is my data private?
Yes — entries are stored only in your browser on this device.

🌐 Does it need internet?
No — the ledger works offline once the page is open.

💾 Do entries stay after I close the tab?
Yes — entries are kept in local storage, so your month's record is still there when you reopen the page.

📐 Quick formulas
Balance = money in − money out this month
Each entry = an amount plus a note
