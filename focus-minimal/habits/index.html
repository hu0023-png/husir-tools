Habits

📘 About this tool

A minimal daily habit tracker that helps you keep a simple streak alive. Add a habit, tap the check circle each day you do it, and the streak bar shows your rhythm — great for morning routines, reading, exercise, and ADHD-friendly tiny goals.

Everything is stored locally in your browser: private, offline, and not tied to any account. Delete habits you no longer need and keep the list as simple as your day.

🔥 How is the streak counted?
Completing the habit on consecutive days extends the streak, shown in days (Nd); the dots give a quick view of recent days.

✅ How do I mark a day?
Tap the check circle beside the habit when you finish it today.

🗑️ Can I remove a habit?
Yes — use the delete button on the habit row.

🔒 Is my data private?
Yes — everything is stored only on this device.

📆 What if I miss a day?
Just tap the circle again tomorrow — the goal is a fresh streak and a simple daily rhythm.
