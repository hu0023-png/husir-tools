Free Pomodoro Timer for Focus — Work and Break Cycles in Your Browser

📘 About this tool

A Pomodoro timer built for deep focus: adjustable Focus and Break minutes, a progress ring, and a session counter. It tracks real elapsed time, so it stays accurate even when the tab is in the background — ideal for study blocks, deep work, and ADHD-friendly pacing.

No signup, no cloud sync, and no distractions — just start a Focus round, work until the ring completes, then take the Break. The session counter shows how much focused time you have accumulated.

⏱️ What is a good starting setup?
Classic Pomodoro is 25 minutes of Focus followed by a 5-minute Break; use the +/− buttons to adjust either round.

🕐 Does it stay accurate in the background?
Yes — the timer measures wall-clock time instead of simple ticking, so hidden tabs stay on schedule.

🔔 How do I know a round ended?
The timer signals the end of the round with a chime and a browser notification when your browser allows it.

🌐 Does it need internet?
No — everything runs offline on your device.

🎯 How many rounds should I do?
Four 25-minute rounds (about two hours of focus) is a common target, but any rhythm you can repeat is a win.
