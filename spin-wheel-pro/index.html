📘 About this tool

A fun prize wheel for draws, giveaways, and everyday decisions: add the items you want, click the center to spin, and the wheel picks one fairly at random. Win history and a prize list help you run events, classroom activities, streams, and party games with confidence.

It works offline and runs entirely on your device — you control the items, and the results never leave your browser.

🎡 Is the spin random?
Yes — each spin picks from the current list using a random draw in your browser, so every item has an equal chance.

✏️ Can I change the items?
Yes — edit the prize list at any time; the wheel always spins from the items you see.

🏆 What if the same person wins twice?
Spin again — or remove that item between spins for draws where each entry should win only once.

🌐 Does it need internet?
No — the wheel works offline once the page is open.

🎲 Can I use it for decisions too?
Absolutely — put options such as restaurants or movies on the wheel and let the spin break the tie.
