💼 Meeting Cost Calculator

Attendees × hourly rate × time

📘 About this tool

Put a price tag on meetings. Enter how many people attend, their average hourly cost, and how long the meeting runs — the calculator shows the true cost of that 30-minute standup or two-hour workshop. It is a classic US workplace exercise for deciding whether a meeting (or an extra attendee) is worth it.

Everything is computed instantly in your browser: no calendar data, no uploads, no accounts. Use the per-minute and per-person numbers to trim invite lists and shorten sessions that drag on.

👥 What hourly cost should I use?
A common shortcut is salary ÷ 2,000 hours (a $100,000 employee costs about $50/hour). For fully loaded cost, add benefits overhead — many teams use salary ÷ 1,000–1,500 to include benefits and overhead.

📉 How can this change behavior?
Seeing that a 1-hour, 10-person meeting costs $750–$1,000 makes it easier to cut the meeting to 30 minutes, drop optional attendees, or replace it with async updates.

🧮 What is the formula?
Meeting cost = attendees × hourly rate × hours. To include setup time, add a few minutes to the duration.

🔒 Is anything uploaded?
No — the calculation runs entirely on your device and works offline.

📐 Key formulas
Cost = attendees × hourly rate × (minutes ÷ 60)
Annual salary → rough hourly = salary ÷ 2,000 working hours
Fully loaded hourly ≈ salary ÷ 1,000–1,500 (benefits + overhead)
