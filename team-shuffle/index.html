💺 Seating Chart Randomizer

Enter names · Groups & Seats & Matchups · All-in-one

📘 About this tool

Turn a list of names into groups, seats, or matchups in one click. Teachers use it for seating charts and breakout teams; trainers and hosts use it for icebreakers, games, and interviews. Enter names, choose a group size or rows/seats, shuffle, and everyone is placed instantly.

Every shuffle runs locally in your browser, so names never leave the device — no signup or data storage. Re-roll as often as you like, then copy or export the results as CSV to paste into a spreadsheet or slide.

🔀 Can I reroll?
Yes — shuffle again for a brand-new arrangement.

👥 Group size or number of groups?
Set your preferred group size and the tool splits the list; the last group may be smaller. Seat mode uses the rows or seats you configure.

📤 How do I share the result?
Copy the output or export CSV and paste it into Google Sheets, Excel, or a slide.

🔒 Are names uploaded?
No — the randomization happens on your device.

♟️ What is matchups mode for?
Pairing participants for debates, games, interviews, or peer feedback.

📐 Quick formulas
Groups needed ≈ N ÷ group size (the last group may be smaller)
Matchups produce one pairing per two people in the shuffled list
CSV export keeps the displayed order for easy pasting
