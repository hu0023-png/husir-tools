🔒Image Compress & Privacy

🔒 Local Processing
📘 About this tool

Shrink images right in your browser so they load faster, fit in emails, and pass the upload limits on shop listings, forms, and messaging apps. Adjust quality, preview the savings, and process several files at once — handy for teachers, sellers, and anyone sharing photos on the web.

Your pictures never leave the device: no upload step, no account, no hidden cloud copy. You can also strip metadata such as camera model and GPS location before exporting, and toggle dark mode for comfortable batch work in the evening.

🎚️ How much can I compress without ruining quality?
For typical photos, a quality setting of 70–85% keeps a good look while cutting file size by roughly 50–80%. The preview shows the trade-off before you save.

📄 Which formats are supported?
The tool processes the image formats your browser can open — commonly JPEG, PNG, and WebP. If a file does not load, try converting it to PNG or JPEG first.

📍 How do I remove location data?
Use the metadata/EXIF removal option before downloading, especially before posting or sending photos.

🎯 What file size should I aim for?
Roughly 100–200 KB for web images, under 25 MB for Gmail attachments, and below whatever limit the shop or form requires.

📐 Quick formulas
Saved space = original size − compressed size
Compression ratio = saved space ÷ original size × 100%
Typical photo: 50–80% smaller at quality 70–85%
