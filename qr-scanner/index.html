🔍 QR Code Scanner

Scan with your camera or an image file · decoded on your device · 100% private & offline

📘 About this tool

Point your camera at a QR code or pick a QR image from your device, and the decoded text appears instantly — a URL, Wi-Fi details, plain text, or whatever the code contains. Unlike most scanner apps, nothing leaves your device: the image never gets uploaded to a server, so you can safely scan codes from emails, printouts, or packaging without a third party logging what you looked at.

Use the camera for physical codes (menus, posters, labels) or the file button for screenshots and downloaded images. Results stay in a list on screen with one-tap copy — close the page and the history is gone, which is exactly what privacy-conscious scanning should feel like. The page works fully offline.

📱 Why does my browser show a message about the camera?
Scanning needs your camera, so the browser asks for permission first. If you blocked it, click the camera icon in the address bar to allow it again.

🧭 Which browsers support on-device decoding?
Chrome and Edge on desktop and Android. Safari and Firefox decode with the built-in camera apps instead — this page needs the browser's native BarcodeDetector feature.

🔒 Is the QR code uploaded?
Never. All decoding runs locally on your device with zero network calls.

🖥️ Can I scan from a screenshot?
Yes — use “Decode from image” and choose the file. It works the same way, entirely offline.
