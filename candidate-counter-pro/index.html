Candidate Vote Counter

✎ Click to edit title

Manual vote entry · Real-time statistics

📘 About this tool

A manual vote counter for live events: add votes to candidates with one tap, watch the distribution update in real time, and see the winner banner when the count settles. The editable title makes it easy to reuse for class votes, office polls, talent shows, and contests.

It runs fully in the browser with data stored locally — no accounts, no uploads, and no network dependency during your event.

➕ How do I count votes?
Use the controls beside each candidate; every tap updates the totals and the distribution instantly.

🏆 How is the winner decided?
The current leader is highlighted while you count; when the poll closes, the candidate with the most votes wins.

🔄 Can I reuse it for another round?
Yes — set up the candidates for the new round and start counting again.

🔒 Is anything uploaded?
No — the counting and results stay entirely on your device.

📤 Can I see the totals clearly?
Yes — total votes and each candidate's share are shown beside the live distribution, and the current leader is highlighted.

📐 Quick formulas
Vote share = candidate votes ÷ total votes × 100%
Total votes = sum of votes across all candidates
Winner = candidate with the highest vote count
