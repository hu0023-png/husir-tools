🔒 Local Processing — files never leave your device
📘 About this tool

A full PDF workbench that runs entirely in your browser: merge files, split pages, delete or rotate pages, clear metadata, add watermarks, signatures, and page numbers, turn images into PDF, and protect files with a password. No uploads, queues, or accounts — documents never leave your device, which matters for contracts, tax returns, and anything with personal data.

Pick a function, add your PDF, check the page preview, and download the output. The same tools work offline, making them useful on school and office networks with restricted internet.

🔒 Are my PDFs really not uploaded?
Correct — the processing runs locally in JavaScript on your device. The tools keep working even with no internet connection.

📏 What file size is supported?
It depends on your device memory. Very large files can be split or optimized first; if a file stalls, try a smaller one.

🧹 Does Clear Metadata remove everything hidden?
It removes common document metadata such as author and software tags. For maximum safety with sensitive content, generate a clean copy before sharing.

✍️ Is an added signature legally valid?
Electronic signatures are accepted for many agreements, but they are not a notarized handwritten signature — check the requirement for your specific document.

🔀 What order will pages be in after merging?
Exactly the order of the files you add — the page preview shows the result before you export.

📐 Quick formulas
After deleting k pages from an N-page PDF: remaining pages = N − k
Watermark readability: 20–40% opacity keeps text underneath visible
Common targets: under 5 MB for email, under 10 MB for most upload forms
