💵 Paycheck Calculator

Hourly ↔ Salary · Federal, state & FICA estimates · Take-home pay

📘 About this tool

See what your paycheck actually looks like after taxes. Convert between an hourly wage and an annual salary, choose how often you are paid (weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, or monthly), and estimate federal, state, and FICA withholding plus a 401(k) contribution to get a realistic take-home amount — all without entering your Social Security number or linking a bank.

Estimates run entirely in your browser. Withholding percentages are editable because every state and situation differs: adjust federal %, state %, and the 401(k) % to match your W-4 and benefits, then use the result for budgeting, job offers, and salary negotiations.

💵 How do I switch between hourly and salary?
Pick Hourly rate or Annual salary at the top. The tool then shows the equivalent for the other basis, so a $30/hr offer is instantly compared to a $62,400/year salary.

🧾 What does FICA include?
FICA combines Social Security (6.2%, capped at the annual wage base) and Medicare (1.45%). The default 7.65% is a reasonable full-year estimate.

🏦 How does 401(k) affect the math?
A 401(k) contribution is taken out before federal, state, and FICA taxes are calculated, which lowers your taxable income — the tool applies it pre-tax.

⚠️ Is this exact?
No — it is an estimator. Real paychecks depend on your W-4, tax bracket, state rules, benefits, and pre-tax deductions. Confirm with your payroll provider.

🔒 Is my income uploaded?
No — everything is calculated locally on your device, and the offline copy needs no internet.

📐 Key formulas
Gross per pay period = annual salary ÷ periods per year (hourly: rate × hours/week × weeks per period)
Taxable = gross − 401(k) contribution
Net pay (take-home) = gross − 401(k) − federal − state − FICA
Common periods per year: weekly 52 · biweekly 26 · semi-monthly 24 · monthly 12

⚠️ Disclaimer
Estimates for planning only — not tax advice. Rates and rules change; verify with your state or a tax professional before filing.
