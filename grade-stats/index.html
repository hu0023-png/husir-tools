📘 About this tool

Paste a list of scores — homework, quizzes, or exams — into this free grade calculator and get an instant class readout: average, median, highest, lowest, pass rate (scores at least 60), a distribution chart, and a ranked list with A–F letter grades and a class GPA on the 4.0 scale. Built for teachers, tutors, and students who want a fast check without spreadsheet formulas.

The analysis runs locally, so scores never leave your browser. Use the overview to spot easy wins, review results before a parent meeting, or export the ranked list as CSV for your gradebook.

🏷️ How are grades grouped?
The default buckets are Excellent ≥90, Good 80–89, Average 70–79, Pass 60–69, and Needs Work below 60; pass rate counts scores ≥60.

🔤 How do letter grades and GPA work?
Unweighted US 4.0 scale: A range 90+ (A = 4.0), B 80–89 (3.0), C 70–79 (2.0), D 60–69 (1.0), F below 60 (0.0), with plus/minus steps in between. Class GPA is the average of each score's 4.0 points.

📋 How do I paste scores?
Copy numbers from a spreadsheet or document and paste them into the box — new lines, spaces, and commas all work.

🔒 Where does my data go?
Nowhere. Everything is calculated in your browser, and closing the tab leaves no copy behind.

📤 Can I export the results?
Yes — export the ranked list as CSV and open it in Excel, Google Sheets, or your gradebook.

❓ What if a student is missing a score?
Only the scores you paste are counted, so simply leave the missing one out.

📐 Quick formulas
Average = sum of scores ÷ number of scores
Median = middle value after sorting (for an even count, the average of the two middle values)
Pass rate = number of scores ≥ 60 ÷ total scores × 100%
Letter (unweighted): A+ 97 · A 93 · A- 90 · B+ 87 · B 83 · B- 80 · C+ 77 · C 73 · C- 70 · D+ 67 · D 63 · D- 60 · F below 60
