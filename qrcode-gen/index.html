📘 About this tool

Create a scannable QR code in seconds — for a URL, plain text, phone number, email address, Wi-Fi login, or vCard contact — then download a PNG to print on posters, menus, business cards, product packaging, door stickers, or classroom handouts. Everything runs locally in your browser: nothing is uploaded, tracked, or stored, so private data such as Wi-Fi passwords never leaves your device. Add a center logo, recolor the modules, and pick an error-correction level before exporting. The generator also works fully offline, which makes it ideal for classrooms, shops, and venues with restricted internet.

For reliable scanning, keep high contrast (dark modules on a light background), leave a quiet zone of four blank modules around the code, and test a small print with your phone camera before mass printing.

🧩 Why won't my QR code scan?
Usually low contrast, a missing quiet zone, or print that is too small. Rule of thumb: minimum code width ≈ viewing distance ÷ 10 (3 cm for a 30 cm scan, 10 cm for a 1 m scan).

🛡️ Which error-correction level should I use?
Levels L, M, Q, and H recover about 7%, 15%, 25%, and 30% of damaged modules. Use M as the everyday default; choose Q or H for a center logo or curved, textured, or fabric surfaces.

🎯 How much data can a QR code hold?
Up to 7,089 digits, 4,296 alphanumeric characters, or 2,953 bytes (Version 40, level L). Shorter content keeps the code compact and easy to scan.

🖼️ Can I put a logo in the middle?
Yes. Choose level Q or H and keep the logo under about 25–30% of the code width so error correction can rebuild the covered modules.

🔓 Do these QR codes expire?
No — QR codes store static data that never expires. URL codes need internet on the scanning phone; text, phone, email, Wi-Fi, and vCard codes work offline.

Modules per side = 17 + 4 × version (Version 1 = 21 × 21, Version 40 = 177 × 177).
