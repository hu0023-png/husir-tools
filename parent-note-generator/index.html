💬 Parent Note Generator

Situation · tone · details · a ready-to-send message in seconds

📘 About this tool

Communicating with families is one of the most time-consuming parts of teaching. This generator turns a situation into a complete, ready-to-send note: pick a topic (positive shout-out, missing work, a behavior conversation, a meeting request, and more), choose a tone, fill in the student and teacher names, and add one specific detail. The message is written for you — warm enough to build trust, clear enough to stay professional.

Teachers use it to send the quick "great day today" email that builds goodwill, to document concerns consistently for the office, or to reply to a parent request without staring at a blank page. The note appears in the preview box where you can still edit anything, then copy it into your school email, an SMS, or a parent-portal message — or open it straight in your email app with the subject filled in. Recent notes are auto-saved on the device so you can resend or reuse them. Everything runs locally: no account, no upload, and it works offline.

🎯 How specific should the "detail" be?
One short concrete sentence works best, e.g. "three missing math assignments since Feb 3" or "helped a classmate during the fire drill". If you leave it blank, the note uses a general version.

😊 Which tone should I pick?
Warm & friendly suits good news and relationship-building. Professional is the safe default for concerns. Concise is for quick texts or busy parents. All three stay respectful and solution-focused.

✏️ Can I edit the message?
Yes — the preview is a normal text box. Your edits stay until you change a setting and regenerate.

💾 Are old notes kept?
The last few generated notes are saved locally and listed under the preview so you can copy them again. Nothing is sent anywhere.

🔒 Is student information uploaded?
No. Names and notes never leave your browser — important when handling student privacy (FERPA-conscious by design).

📐 Quick reference
Message = greeting + opening + specific detail + action/next step + closing + signature.
Good practice: start positive, state the fact, end with an invitation to talk ("feel free to reach out").
Keep it under ~150 words for email; under ~60 words for SMS.
