📶 WiFi QR Code Generator

Guests scan with their phone camera and join Wi-Fi instantly — printable free-Wi-Fi card

📘 About this tool

Turn your Wi-Fi into a scannable sign. This generator creates a QR code that lets guests connect by pointing their phone camera at it — no typing the network name or password. It is ideal for cafes, restaurants, hotels, salons, barbershops, Airbnb hosts, waiting rooms, and offices that want visitors online in seconds.

Everything is encoded on your device: SSID, security type, and password are never uploaded to any server. Style the code with brand colors, add an optional center logo (great for door stickers and marketing), then download a PNG or print a ready-made Free Wi-Fi card with your store name.

📱 Do guests need to install an app?
No. The iPhone Camera app and most Android cameras (Google Lens and other built-in scanners) read Wi-Fi QR codes natively.

❌ Why won't a phone connect?
Make sure the Security type matches your router (WPA2/WPA3, WEP, or Open/None) and that the password is exactly right — QR Wi-Fi values are case-sensitive.

🔄 Can I change the password later?
Yes — edit the settings and regenerate. Guests then need the new code, so update your printed signs too.

🏷️ Is it safe to share my Wi-Fi password this way?
Anyone who scans receives the network password, so use this on a guest network or a connection you are comfortable sharing with customers.

🎨 Why won't it scan with a logo in the middle?
The logo covers modules. Raise the error-correction level (Q or H) and keep the logo under about 25–30% of the code width.

📐 Quick formulas
Wi-Fi payload: WIFI:T:WPA;S:<SSID>;P:<password>;H:true;; (add H:true only for a hidden network)
Error correction recovers about L 7% · M 15% · Q 25% · H 30% of damaged modules
Print size rule: minimum code width ≈ viewing distance ÷ 10 (a 10 cm card reads from about 1 m)
