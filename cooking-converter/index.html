🥣 Cooking & Baking Converter

Grams ↔ cups for real ingredients · oven °F ↔ °C · runs 100% in your browser

📘 About this tool

American recipes measure with cups and spoons, while most of the world bakes by weight — and one cup of flour is never the same as one cup of sugar. This converter turns any amount of a real ingredient into every unit you might need: grams, kilograms, ounces, pounds, US cups, tablespoons, teaspoons, and fluid ounces. Pick the ingredient, type an amount, and all conversions update instantly using a standard weight per cup for that ingredient.

It also handles the two details that trip up international recipes: butter sticks (one US stick is 113 g, or ½ cup) and oven temperatures, with an instant °F ↔ °C converter. Everything is calculated locally on your device — no sign-up, no uploads, fully offline, and safe to open with your own recipe in hand.

🥣 Why is 1 cup of flour not 1 cup of sugar in grams?
Weight per cup depends on density. A US cup of all-purpose flour is about 120 g (spooned), while granulated sugar is about 200 g per cup.

🧀 How many grams are in a stick of butter?
One US stick = 113 g = 8 tablespoons = ½ cup. Two sticks = 1 cup = 227 g.

🧮 Should I spoon or scoop flour into the cup?
Spoon it in and level it off. Scooping can add 20–30% more flour than the 120 g standard.

🔥 What is 350 °F in Celsius?
350 °F = 177 °C. General rule: subtract 32, multiply by 5, divide by 9.

📏 Is a US cup the same as a metric cup?
Not quite. A US cup is 236.6 mL; many metric recipes use a 250 mL cup. This tool uses standard US cup measures.

📐 Quick reference
Grams = cups × grams-per-cup(ingredient)  ·  1 US cup = 16 tbsp = 48 tsp = 8 fl oz = 236.6 mL
°C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9  ·  Butter: 1 stick = 113 g = ½ cup
