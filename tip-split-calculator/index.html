Tip & Split

Quick calc · No ads · Works offline
📘 About this tool

Split restaurant and service bills without the awkward math. Enter the bill amount, choose a tip percentage from the common presets or set your own, and say how many people are paying. See the tip total, the full bill, and exactly what each person owes — with rounding options when you want clean numbers for cash.

It runs instantly in the browser, needs no account, and works offline — handy at dinner tables, group trips, and anywhere a group splits a check.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Estimates for planning only — not tax or financial advice. Rates and rules change, so verify with your state or a qualified professional before filing or making big decisions.

💰 What tip percentage should I use?
In the United States, 15% is a common baseline, 18–20% for good service; presets cover these, and you can type any custom amount.

🧮 Should I tip on the pre-tax or after-tax amount?
In the US the usual practice is tipping on the pre-tax bill. Enter a sales-tax rate (optional) and switch on "Tip on total (incl. tax)" to tip on the after-tax amount instead.

💵 How does rounding work?
Use the rounding option to get clean per-person amounts, which is convenient when paying cash.

👥 Can I split unevenly?
This tool splits the total equally among the people you set; for uneven shares, run the calculation for each share separately.

🔒 Does it need internet?
No — the calculation happens instantly on your device, even offline.

📐 Quick formulas
Tip = tip base × tip% (pre-tax: bill only · incl. tax: bill + tax)
Total = bill + tax + tip
Per person = total ÷ number of people
Example: 18% on a $54.20 bill with 8% tax and pre-tax tip → $9.76 tip, $68.30 total, $22.77 each for 3
