🎨 Color Contrast & Palette

WCAG contrast checker · Complementary / triadic / shades

📘 About this tool

Two color tools in one page, built for designers and developers: a WCAG contrast checker that tests text against any background, and a palette generator that creates complementary, triadic, analogous, and shade schemes from one brand color. Every result includes copy-ready hex codes.

Contrast matters for accessibility — low-contrast text is hard to read and fails WCAG 2.x. The checker shows the ratio (from 1:1 to 21:1) and whether the pair passes AA and AAA for normal and large text. All color math happens locally; no images or colors are uploaded.

🧑‍🦯 What does WCAG require?
For normal text: AA needs 4.5:1 and AAA needs 7:1. Large text (18px+ or 14px bold) passes AA at 3:1 and AAA at 4.5:1.

🧮 How is contrast calculated?
Contrast = (Lighter + 0.05) ÷ (Darker + 0.05), using the WCAG relative-luminance formula for each color's red, green, and blue channels.

🎨 What do the palette schemes mean?
Complementary is the opposite color (180°), triadic uses three evenly spaced hues (120° apart), analogous steps to nearby hues, and shades keeps one hue while changing lightness.

📋 How do I copy a color?
Click any palette chip — its hex code is copied to your clipboard instantly.

🔒 Is anything uploaded?
No — all calculations run locally on your device and the page works offline.

📐 Key formulas
Contrast ratio = (L₁ + 0.05) ÷ (L₂ + 0.05), where L is WCAG relative luminance
Passes AA: normal 4.5:1 · large 3:1 · AAA: normal 7:1 · large 4.5:1
Palette hues: complementary 180° · triadic 120° · analogous ±30–60°
