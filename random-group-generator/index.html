👥 Random Group Generator

Paste names · choose team size or team count · shuffle · copy

📘 About this tool

Turn a class list, team roster, or group of friends into fair random teams in one click. Paste names, choose either a team size (for example 4 people per team) or a fixed number of teams, and the tool shuffles everyone before splitting, so no draw favors any name. Groups stay as balanced as possible: with 23 students and 5 teams you get 5, 5, 5, 4, and 4 members.

Use it for classroom group work, icebreakers, study pods, project teams, sports drills, or workshop breakouts. Everything runs in the browser — names never leave your device, which keeps student rosters private. Re-shuffle as often as you like, then copy the final teams as plain text for a document, slide, or chat message.

🎲 Is the shuffle really random?
Yes — every run uses the Fisher–Yates algorithm, which gives every possible ordering an equal chance. Each new shuffle is independent of the last.

⚖️ Why are my teams not exactly equal?
When names do not divide evenly, the remainder is spread one per team from the start. For example, 22 names into 5 teams gives 5, 5, 4, 4, and 4 members.

👥 Team size or number of teams — which should I pick?
Use team size when you want small pods (for example "groups of 4"); use number of teams when you want a fixed count for stations or rotations.

🔒 Are student names uploaded?
No. The page runs fully in your browser and stores nothing — close the tab and the names are gone.

📋 How do I share the final teams?
Click "Copy teams" and paste the numbered list into a slide, worksheet, or message. Results are also shown on screen for projecting.

📐 Quick formulas
Total names N = non-empty lines (duplicates removed once).
Team size S: teams = ceil(N ÷ S).
T teams: base = floor(N ÷ T); remainder R = N mod T → the first R teams get base + 1 members.
Always shuffle before splitting so every assignment stays random.
