📘 About this tool

Make playful social-media flex posts in seconds — eight ready-made templates that turn a funny idea into a shareable image. They are great for harmless jokes among friends, group chats, and light entertainment content. Keep it light: the output is clearly meant for entertainment only.

Everything runs locally in your browser — no upload, no account, no watermark. Pick a template, fill in your text, preview the result, and download or share the image.

🤡 Can I use this to fool people?
No. These are for entertainment only — do not use the images to misrepresent real documents, purchases, or achievements.

🧩 How many templates are there?
Eight different layouts are included; click through them to see which one fits your joke.

📤 How do I save the result?
Download the finished image as PNG and share it anywhere you like.

🔒 Is my text uploaded?
No — the image is generated locally on your device.

💡 What makes a good flex post?
Short, funny captions work best — the template handles the layout while your text carries the joke. Preview first, and never include real personal or financial details.
