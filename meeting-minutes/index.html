Meeting Minutes Generator

Paste meeting transcripts — auto-extract key points, action items, and decisions.
Optimized for Chinese-language meetings. English support is experimental.

📘 About this tool

Turn a raw meeting transcript into structured minutes: a meeting summary, key decisions, action items, and keywords. Paste the transcript (Chinese or English) or use the optional speech-to-text step, then export a formatted Word document or copy the result.

The extraction runs locally in your browser, so confidential discussions — board calls, client meetings, and HR talks — are never uploaded for processing. It is quick enough to run right after a call while the context is still fresh.

🌐 Which languages does it support?
Chinese and English transcripts are supported, and the tool is tuned for Chinese-language meetings in particular.

📤 How do I get the minutes out?
Use the export template to download a Word-style file, or copy the formatted text into email or a document.

🔒 Is my transcript uploaded?
No — processing happens on your device, and closing the tab leaves nothing behind.

✏️ Can I correct mistakes?
Yes — edit the transcript and regenerate, then review the extracted sections before exporting.

🎙️ What if I have no transcript yet?
Use the optional speech-to-text step to get a draft, then paste it in for summarization.
