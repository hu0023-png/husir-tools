📐 Social Media Image Resizer

One upload · pick a platform size · zoom & drag · export PNG or JPG

📘 About this tool

Posting the same photo everywhere? Every platform crops and zooms differently, so one picture rarely fits all. This tool resizes and crops one image to the exact pixel dimensions each platform expects — Instagram post, portrait, story or reel, Facebook post, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Pinterest, and YouTube thumbnails — so you can publish once and look sharp everywhere.

Choose a preset (or type your own pixels), pick Cover to crop and fill the frame or Contain to show the whole photo with a background color, then zoom and drag to choose exactly which part of the picture shows. Everything runs locally: your image is never uploaded, which makes it safe for product shots, event photos, or client content.

🖼 Which size should I use?
Common safe sizes: Instagram post 1080×1080, portrait 1080×1350, story/reel 1080×1920, Facebook & LinkedIn post 1200×630, X/Twitter 1600×900, Pinterest 1000×1500, YouTube thumbnail 1280×720. Using larger pixels than the minimum keeps quality on retina screens.

📈 Does enlarging a small image keep it sharp?
No — upscaling only stretches existing pixels. Start from a source at least as large as the target for the cleanest result; the tool warns you when your photo is smaller than the target.

🖌 Cover or Contain — what is the difference?
Cover scales the photo to fill the whole frame and crops the overflow (best for posts). Contain fits the entire photo inside with matching bars on the sides (best for stories where nothing may be cut).

✋ Can I move the crop?
Yes — drag the photo inside the preview to reposition it and use the zoom buttons or your scroll wheel to frame the subject exactly.

🔒 Is my photo uploaded?
No. The file is processed only in your browser and never leaves your device.

📐 Quick formulas
Frame pixels = width × height (Instagram story = 1080 × 1920).
Aspect ratio = width ÷ height (1080 × 1350 = 4:5 portrait).
Cover scale = max(targetW ÷ sourceW, targetH ÷ sourceH); Contain scale = min(...).
For sharp exports keep source ≥ target and export JPG at 85–92% quality.
