🏦 Loan Calculator

Mortgage & Auto · Monthly payment + amortization table

📘 About this tool

Figure out what a loan really costs before you sign — a 30-year mortgage, a 15-year refi, a new car loan, or a personal loan. Enter the amount you are borrowing, the annual interest rate (APR), and the term; the calculator returns the monthly payment, total interest, total cost, and a month-by-month amortization table showing how each payment splits between interest and principal.

It runs 100% in your browser — no bank account, no credit check, no upload. Use it to compare loan offers, test extra terms, and see how a lower rate or a shorter term changes the total interest you pay.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Estimates for planning only — not tax or financial advice. Rates and rules change, so verify with your state or a qualified professional before filing or making big decisions.

🏠 What should I enter as the loan amount?
Use the amount you will actually finance after your down payment — for a house, that is the price minus down payment; for a car, the out-the-door price minus trade-in and down payment.

📉 Does APR include fees?
APR includes most lender fees by definition. The tool uses the APR you type, so the result is a close estimate; property tax, insurance, and PMI are not included.

🧾 How is the monthly payment calculated?
With the standard amortization formula: M = P × [r(1+r)ⁿ] ÷ [(1+r)ⁿ − 1], where P is the principal, r is the monthly rate (APR ÷ 12), and n is the number of months.

📅 What is an amortization table?
It shows each month's payment split: early payments are mostly interest, and later payments are mostly principal — the table scrolls so you can inspect any month.

🔒 Is my information uploaded?
No — all calculations happen locally on your device, and the offline copy needs no internet.

📐 Key formulas
Monthly payment M = P × [r(1+r)ⁿ] ÷ [(1+r)ⁿ − 1] (r = APR ÷ 12, n = months)
Total interest = M × n − P
Total cost = M × n = principal + total interest
