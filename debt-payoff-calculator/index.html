📉 Debt Payoff Calculator

Snowball vs avalanche · Debt-free date · Interest saved with extra payments

📘 About this tool

Credit card balances, student loans, car notes, personal loans — when several debts carry different balances and interest rates, the order you pay them matters. This calculator compares the two classic strategies: the snowball method (smallest balance first, for quick wins and motivation) and the avalanche method (highest APR first, which usually saves the most interest). Add an extra monthly payment and it shows your projected debt-free date, total interest under each strategy, and exactly how much extra you save versus paying only the minimums.

Enter each debt's balance, APR, and minimum payment, pick a strategy, and adjust the "extra per month" slider-style field until the timeline feels right. All math runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded, and you never need an account. The result is a payoff plan you can follow month by month; actual savings depend on keeping rates and minimums unchanged.

❄️ Snowball or avalanche — which is better?
Avalanche minimizes total interest; snowball builds momentum by retiring the smallest balance first. Both keep your monthly payment the same — only the extra dollar goes to a different debt. Pick the one you will actually stick with.

💳 Should I pay more than the minimum?
Minimums are designed to stretch repayment for years. Even $50–$100 extra a month can shave years off a high-APR card and save hundreds or thousands in interest.

🧮 How is monthly interest calculated?
Interest accrues daily but is quoted annually. This tool approximates monthly interest as balance × APR ÷ 12, which is accurate enough for planning.

🔄 What happens when a debt is paid off?
Its minimum payment rolls onto the next debt in your chosen order, which is why the snowball accelerates as balances disappear.

📅 What if I only pay minimums forever?
You may never pay off high-interest cards. If the calculator shows no debt-free date, increase the extra payment or attack the highest-APR balance first.

📐 Quick formulas
Monthly interest = balance × APR ÷ 12
Monthly payment stays fixed = sum of minimums + extra
Avalanche targets: highest APR → Snowball targets: lowest balance
Total interest = total paid − original balances
