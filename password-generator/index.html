🔐 Password Generator

Random & strong · Length + character sets + strength meter

📘 About this tool

Create strong, random passwords and passphrases right in your browser — no server, no clipboard spyware, no account. Choose the length, pick which character sets to include (lowercase, uppercase, numbers, symbols), and optionally exclude confusing characters like 1, l, I, 0, and O. Every password is generated locally with the browser's secure random source, and the strength meter estimates how hard it would be to brute-force.

Use it for new accounts, password-manager master passwords, and one-off codes. Because nothing ever leaves your device, you can generate as many as you like without creating a trail.

🔤 What length should I use?
For most accounts, 12–16 characters is a solid default; use 16+ for important accounts like email, banking, and your password manager.

🎲 How is strength measured?
Entropy (in bits) ≈ length × log₂(pool size). A 12-character password from all 94 printable symbols has about 78 bits of entropy — far too many combinations to try by brute force.

👀 Why avoid similar characters?
Characters like 0/O, 1/l/I look alike on paper and in fonts; excluding them makes passwords easier to read aloud and retype from a printed sheet.

🔁 Should I reuse passwords?
No — reuse is how one leaked site exposes other accounts. Generate a unique password per site and store them in a password manager.

🔒 Is the password uploaded anywhere?
Never. Generation runs entirely on your device, and the page works offline with zero network calls.
