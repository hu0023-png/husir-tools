🏆 March Madness Bracket Generator

Seeded tournament bracket · pick winners round by round · runs 100% in your browser

📘 About this tool

Every March, 64 college teams get a bracket and millions of people fill one out in ten minutes. This generator builds a standard seeded single-elimination bracket — 16-team region or the full 64-team field — with the classic 1-vs-16 matchups, then lets you click through each round to crown a champion.

Start with the fun: it fills the field with familiar program names (or 8–64 of your own if you want a different game), then pick winners by tapping teams in each round. When both teams in a matchup are decided, the winner advances automatically into the next column. If you just want a finished sheet fast, press Auto-play and it simulates the entire tournament to a champion, then Reset to try again.

Everything happens on your device — no account, no upload, works fully offline. Use it for an office pool, a classroom demo of bracket math, or just to feel the chaos before the real games tip off.

🏆 How are the seeds arranged?
The classic pairing: in each 16-team region, round one pairs seeds 1–16, 8–9, 5–12, 4–13, 6–11, 3–14, 7–10, and 2–15. Winners advance to play adjacent matchups.

🎯 Why are some teams grayed out?
A later-round matchup only opens once both teams in the two games feeding it are decided. Pick earlier games first, or just Auto-play the whole bracket.

📋 Can I use my own teams?
Yes — uncheck “Fill with college teams”, enter your own list (one team per line), and generate. Any list that is a power of two (8, 16, 32, 64) works.

🔒 Is my bracket saved or sent anywhere?
No. It exists only on this page for this session. Refresh and you start fresh.

📐 Bracket math
A bracket with N teams has log₂(N) rounds (64 → 32 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 2 → 1 champion) and N−1 total games.

🏆 Tournament

Press New bracket to build a field, then click winners to advance.

Click a team to pick it as the winner. Later rounds unlock as earlier games are decided.
