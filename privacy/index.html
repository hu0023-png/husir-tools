🔒 Privacy Policy

HuSir Tools · Last updated: September 2026

HuSir Tools is designed to minimize data collection. Our tools are single-file web pages that run in your browser. The files, text, grades, photos, and numbers you work with are processed locally on your own device and are not uploaded to us.

1. What the tools collect

Nothing, by design. When you open a tool page and use it, your content stays in your browser session. Closing the tab removes it. Some tools store small preferences (such as a theme choice or a saved draft) in your browser's local storage so the page remembers them next time — that data never leaves your device either.

2. What happens when you visit this website

Like any public website, this site is served through a hosting provider and a content delivery network (Cloudflare). Standard server logs may record technical details such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, for security and reliability. These logs are not used to build profiles about you.

3. Payments and donations

Optional purchases and donations are handled by third-party services (such as Gumroad or Buy Me a Coffee). When you use them, their own privacy policies apply. HuSir Tools does not receive or store your card details.

4. Email

If you email us, we see only the message you choose to send. We do not add you to any mailing list without your consent.

5. Your choices

You can clear a tool's saved data at any time using your browser's site-data or storage settings. If you have questions about this policy, contact hu0023@gmail.com.

This page may be updated as the site evolves. Material changes will be dated above.
