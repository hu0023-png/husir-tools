⏳ Countdown & Event Timer

Count down to anything · US federal holiday presets · saved on your device

📘 About this tool

Track how long until the moments that matter: a birthday, vacation, product launch, wedding, deadline, or the next holiday. Add as many events as you like — each gets a name, a date, and an optional time — then watch the days, hours, minutes, and seconds tick down in real time. Your list is saved automatically in this browser, so it is still there when you come back.

For people in the United States, one-click presets add all ten federal holidays for 2026 or 2027 — New Year’s Day, MLK Day, Presidents’ Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas — calculated from the real calendar rules (for example, Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday of November). Everything runs locally on your device; no account, no upload, fully offline.

📅 How are the federal holiday dates calculated?
Each holiday follows its legal rule — for example MLK Day is the third Monday of January, Memorial Day the last Monday of May, and Thanksgiving the fourth Thursday of November. Fixed-date holidays like July 4 stay on their date.

🕒 Why does a countdown show an extra day near midnight?
Countdowns run to the exact time you set (default midnight at the start of the date). If you add an event the evening before, it can show less than one day remaining — set the time to 11:59 PM to count to the end of that day.

🔁 Do events stay after I close the page?
Yes — events are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). Clearing your browser data will remove them; nothing is ever uploaded.

💪 What happens when an event passes?
The card flips to a muted “passed” state showing the date it happened. Delete it with the ✕ button or keep it as a record.

🔒 Does this timer send my data anywhere?
Never. Countdown math runs locally every second with zero network calls.
