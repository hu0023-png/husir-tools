🍎 Classroom Privacy

8 free teacher tools that never upload student data

Grades, seating charts, attendance, rubrics, quizzes and parent notes are exactly the data a school should be careful with. Every teacher tool on this site runs inside your browser. Student names and scores are typed into your machine, calculated on your machine, and saved on your machine. There is no account, no class roster on a server, and no upload step — not even a file picker that could send one.

The claim, stated exactly: student names, scores, attendance marks and notes are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Nothing is stored on a server, because nothing is sent to one. And you do not have to take our word for it — the next section shows you how to check it in about a minute.

How to verify this yourself (about 1 minute)

  1. Open a tool that takes student data — the Grade Stats & Analysis or the Attendance Tracker page.
  2. Open Developer Tools (F12, or Cmd‑Opt‑I on a Mac) and switch to the Network tab. Leave it open.
  3. Type a few rows of data — invent names if you like: scores, attendance marks, whatever the tool asks for.
  4. Watch the request list while you work. No request carries what you typed. The list stays empty of your data even as the numbers on screen update.
  5. Optional but convincing: turn off Wi‑Fi, reload, and do it again. The tool keeps working, because the computation is happening on your machine.
What you will see in that list. The page itself arrives over the network, and a few tools download an open-source JavaScript library (for example from cdn.jsdelivr.net) the first time they run. Those requests carry none of your data — they are the program loading, not student records leaving. If you would rather avoid them entirely, use the offline ZIP below: it has every library built in.

The classroom tools, and what stays local

  • Grade Stats & Analysis — scores, averages, letter grades, class GPA
  • Attendance Tracker — daily present / late / absent / excused roll
  • Seating Chart Randomizer — the student name list itself
  • Random Group Generator — names split into fair teams
  • Classroom Toolkit — name picker, group points, timer, math quiz
  • Quiz & Flashcard Maker — your question sets and decks
  • Rubric Generator — criteria, levels and scoring weights
  • Parent Note Generator — the message you are drafting to a family

All eight keep everything in your browser's local storage on that one device. Nothing is shared between devices, which is the point: a roster typed on your classroom laptop is not readable from anywhere else.

It also works when the network does not

Each tool is one self-contained HTML file, so the whole collection ships as a single ZIP. Unzip it anywhere and open any file — it runs with no connection at all. That matters for real classrooms: a network that blocks uploads, a filter that blocks a new site during class, a substitute's machine, or a room where the Wi‑Fi simply drops.

  • No installation and no admin rights — it is just files.
  • Copy it to a USB stick or a school laptop once, and it works the same all year.

What we deliberately do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email required — for teachers or students.
  • No rosters or grades on a server: there is nowhere they could be kept, because they are never sent.
  • No advertising, no third-party analytics profiles, no cross-site tracking pixels.
  • No watermarks, trial limits or export paywalls on the classroom tools.
  • No "connect your Google Classroom / SIS" integration — that would require the access this whole approach avoids.
Where this fits, honestly. This page is not a FERPA, COPPA or state-compliance certification, and you should not file it as one. What it does is remove one specific risk that district reviews usually raise: student data being uploaded to a third-party service. The tools never transmit it. Whether that is enough is your district's call, so run it past your tech coordinator — this page is written to be printed or forwarded for exactly that conversation.

Questions

Do my students need accounts?
No. There is no sign-up, no login, no class code and no email required — for you or for students.
Where is the data stored?
In your own browser's local storage on that device. Nothing is transmitted. Clearing the browser's site data erases it, so export or print before you clear it.
Is this FERPA compliant?
This page is not a compliance certification. What is factual is that the tools do not transmit student data anywhere, which removes the upload and third-party-disclosure risk that district reviews usually focus on. Your district's rules and your own judgment decide the rest.
Does it work when the school network blocks uploads, or the Wi‑Fi is down?
Yes. Most tools keep working with no connection at all, and the downloadable ZIP needs no connection ever. It can be copied to a USB stick or a school laptop with no installation and no admin rights.
Do the tools work on Chromebooks and iPads?
They are plain web pages, so anything with a current browser works. The offline ZIP is opened from a file rather than a website, which is easiest on a laptop or desktop.
Can I share these with other teachers?
Yes, and you do not need to ask. Send them this page or hand over the ZIP.
Is there advertising or tracking?
No ads and no third-party analytics profiles. The hosting provider and CDN keep standard server logs for security and reliability.
