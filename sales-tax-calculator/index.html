🧾 Sales Tax Calculator

By state · State rate + local rate · Total price

📘 About this tool

Add the right sales tax to any purchase — whether you are a shopper checking a receipt, a freelancer quoting a price, or a small seller setting product totals for US customers. Pick the state, enter the pre-tax amount, add any local (county or city) rate you know applies, and see the tax and the final price instantly.

The calculator is completely local: no forms, no accounts, and no purchase history stored anywhere. State rates are built in and editable via the Local % field, because city and county taxes vary widely even inside one state.

🗺️ Why is my total different from the cash register?
Most states add local taxes on top of the state rate, and some items (groceries, clothing, digital goods) are exempt or taxed differently. Add the local rate that applies to your ZIP code, and remember not every item is taxable.

🟢 Which states have no sales tax?
Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire, and Oregon have no statewide sales tax (Alaska and Montana still allow local taxes in some places).

🧾 Are rates current?
The built-in values are the standard statewide base rates. Local rates and occasional state changes are your responsibility to verify before filing or quoting.

💻 Do sellers need to collect tax?
Usually yes — after economic nexus, most online sellers must collect tax for states where they have significant sales. This tool helps you figure out the amount to charge, not your legal obligation.

🔒 Is anything uploaded?
No — every calculation runs in your browser, and the offline copy works with no internet.

📐 Key formulas
Tax = price × (state rate + local rate)
Total price = price + tax
Effective rate = tax ÷ price × 100%
Example: $100 at CA 7.25% + 2% local = $9.25 tax, $109.25 total

⚠️ Disclaimer
Estimates for planning only — not tax advice. Rates and rules change; verify with your state or a tax professional before filing.
