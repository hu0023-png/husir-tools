🏈 Fantasy Football Draft Kit

Team name generator · snake draft order randomizer

📘 About this tool

Draft day has two chores that can eat a whole evening: picking a funny team name and arguing about who picks first. This kit does both in one page. The team-name generator blends evergreen football puns with your league-mates' names (add your own for custom roast material), then the draft-order randomizer runs a true Fisher–Yates shuffle so the No. 1 pick is decided by luck, not by who shouted loudest.

Every standard league drafts in a snake: the manager with pick 1 also gets pick 2N in round two, while the last pick in round 1 picks first in round 2 — the tool shows that reverse order automatically. Everything runs locally: no account, no app, no upload, works offline at the draft table.

🏆 How does a snake draft order work?
With 10 teams, round 1 runs 1 → 10 and round 2 runs 10 → 1. The tool lists both directions so everyone can see their first-round and second-round picks at a glance.

🎲 Is the draft order really random?
Yes — the Fisher–Yates shuffle gives every ordering an equal chance, so a draw can't be rigged or accidentally biased.

😄 Where do the team names come from?
The generator mixes a library of football puns with owner names you enter. Save the ones you like and copy them to your league site or group chat.

🔒 Is anything uploaded?
No. Names and draft results are processed entirely in your browser and never sent anywhere.

📋 Can I share the results?
Yes — copy the numbered draft order or the saved team names as plain text for your league group.

📐 Quick formulas
Snake: pick P in round R (1-based) = (R odd) ? P : (N + 1 − P).
Draft spot 1 gets the "1.01" label, spot 5 in a 10-team league gets "1.05" then "2.06".
Fisher–Yates: swap each position i with a random j ≥ i, from the end backwards.
