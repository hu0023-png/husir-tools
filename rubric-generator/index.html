📋 Rubric Generator

Criteria & levels · auto descriptors · weighted total · print or copy

📘 About this tool

Build a clean, printable scoring rubric for essays, projects, presentations, labs, or group work — without starting from a blank table. Start from a ready-made level scale (4-level Exceeds/Meets/Developing/Begins, 3-level, or 100-point scale), type in the criteria you are grading, and this tool writes a starter descriptor for every cell using your criterion and level names. Edit any cell in the live preview, or click "Fill generic descriptors" to start each box fresh.

Each criterion can carry a weight, and the footer row shows the maximum score and its share of the total automatically — so a 6-criterion project rubric still adds up to the grade you announced. When the table looks right, print it (or save to PDF from the print dialog) to hand out with the assignment, or copy the text version to paste into Google Docs, Canvas, or an LMS. Everything runs locally in the browser — no account, no upload, and your rubric is auto-saved so a closed tab does not lose your work.

🎯 What is a rubric, and when would I use one?
A rubric is a scoring guide that lists each grading criterion (rows) and describes what different performance levels look like (columns). Use it for any assignment where several teachers or graders need to score consistently — essays, science labs, oral presentations, group projects.

⚖️ How do weights work?
Each row has a weight box. The maximum points shown in the footer are weight × highest level points, and the "% of total" is that value divided by the sum of all weighted maxima. Equal weights are the default.

✏️ Can I change a descriptor after it is generated?
Yes. Every cell in the preview is directly editable — click into a box and type. Only boxes you edit yourself are kept when you click "Fill generic descriptors" again.

💾 Will my rubric be saved?
Yes, the current rubric auto-saves in your browser's local storage. Use Print → Save as PDF when you want a file to attach to an assignment or upload to your LMS.

🔒 Is anything uploaded or shared?
No. The whole tool runs on your device. Rubrics never leave your browser.

📐 Quick reference
Criterion points = weight × level points.
Total = Σ (weight × level points) over all criteria.
Percent of total = (weight × level points) ÷ total × 100.
Example: 4 levels × weight 2 × 4 pts max → 8 pts for that criterion.
