Secret Santa

Random pairings · One-click reveal · Privacy first
📘 About this tool

Organize a fair Secret Santa gift exchange in minutes. Enter the participant names, generate random pairings, and reveal them for your party — everyone gives one gift and receives one. Perfect for office parties, family gatherings, and friend groups.

The page works locally and is designed for one shared screen or a private reveal: your names are never uploaded. Re-shuffle until the drawing feels right, then click reveal when the fun starts.

🎲 How are pairings created?
The shuffle pairs the names randomly so that each participant both gives and receives once.

🔁 Can I shuffle again?
Yes — generate a new set of pairings as many times as you like before you reveal.

👥 What if there is an odd number of people?
Everyone still receives a gift, but one pairing circle may be uneven — some groups add a host or extra participant to even things out.

📤 Can I keep the result?
Use the export option to keep the pairings for later, then reveal when your group is together.

🔒 Are names uploaded?
No — the drawing happens entirely in your browser.

📐 Quick reference
Each run: one giver per participant, one gift received
