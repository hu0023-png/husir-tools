📘 About this tool

Make an ID or passport photo from a selfie with this free online ID photo editor: upload a photo, crop to the right head-and-shoulders framing, replace the background with the color you need, then export in standard sizes — including the US 2×2 passport size and common international visa dimensions — on a ready-to-print sheet.

The whole workflow — crop, background change, beautify, layout, and export — runs in your browser with no upload, so your photo never leaves your device. Handy before passport renewals, visa applications, school IDs, and job paperwork.

📏 Which sizes are included?
Standard presets such as the US 2×2-inch passport size (51×51 mm) and common 35×45 mm visa dimensions are included; always double-check the issuing authority's current requirements.

🎨 What background should I use?
Choose white or the specific color your application requires — plain, evenly lit backgrounds are the norm for official photos.

🖨️ How do I print it?
The layout tab arranges copies on a sheet; print at 100% scale (no fit-to-page) so the size stays exact.

🧾 Will it be accepted everywhere?
Official photo rules change and vary by country — compare your output with the latest requirements, and use a professional service if you are unsure.

🖼️ Can I edit an existing ID photo?
Yes — upload the photo you already have and use this ID photo editor to re-crop it, change the background, adjust the brightness, and export a print-ready sheet in passport, visa, or ID size.

🔒 Is my photo uploaded?
No — all editing happens locally in your browser.
