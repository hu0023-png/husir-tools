🚗 Mileage Tracker

IRS mileage log · Business / Medical / Charitable

📘 About this tool

Log every deductible trip through the year — business errands, medical appointments, and charitable driving — and let the standard IRS mileage rates turn miles into a deduction. Add trips one at a time with a date, type, and note; the tracker totals each category and shows the estimated deduction using editable per-mile rates.

Everything is stored locally in your browser (or nowhere, if you prefer to export and clear). Use the CSV export to drop the log into your tax software or spreadsheet at tax time.

🚗 What is the IRS standard mileage rate?
The IRS sets a cents-per-mile rate each year (current, Jul 1 – Dec 31, 2026: 76¢ business, 23.5¢ medical, 14¢ charitable). Rates are editable here so the tool works for any tax year.

🧾 What counts as business mileage?
Generally, miles driven for work that your employer does not reimburse — visiting clients, running errands, and traveling between work sites. Commuting to your regular workplace usually does not count.

🩺 Medical and charitable mileage?
Medical mileage covers trips to doctors, pharmacies, and treatments; charitable mileage covers driving for a qualified charity. Both have lower rates than business miles.

📁 Do I need to keep records?
Yes — the IRS expects contemporaneous records (date, purpose, miles). This log gives you that paper trail; keep receipts for parking and tolls too.

🔒 Is my data uploaded?
No — entries stay in your browser's local storage on this device, and the page works fully offline.

📐 Key formulas
Deduction = miles × rate for that trip type
Total deduction = business + medical + charitable totals
Current IRS standard rates (Jul 1 – Dec 31, 2026): business $0.76 · medical $0.235 · charitable $0.14 (per mile, editable)

⚠️ Disclaimer
Estimates for planning only — not tax advice. Rates and rules change; verify with your state or a tax professional before filing.
