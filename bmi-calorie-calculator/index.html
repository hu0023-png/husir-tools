⚖️ BMI & Calorie Calculator

BMI + healthy range + BMR + maintenance calories · US or metric · runs 100% in your browser

📘 About this tool

Enter your height, weight, age, and sex to see three numbers at once: your body-mass index (BMI) with its category, your healthy weight range for that height, and the calories your body needs every day just to exist (BMR) plus the daily maintenance calories for your activity level. Switch between US (feet/inches + pounds) and metric (cm + kg) at any time.

BMI is a quick population-level screening tool used by doctors, insurers, and fitness apps; the calorie estimates use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the formula recommended by the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics for healthy adults. Use maintenance calories as your starting point whether your goal is weight loss, gain, or simply eating enough to fuel training. Everything is computed locally on your device — no account, no upload, fully offline.

⚖️ What is a healthy BMI?
For most adults, 18.5–24.9 is considered normal. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30+ is obesity. Children and teens use age-and-sex percentile charts, not these cutoffs.

💪 Why does BMI say I am “overweight” even though I lift weights?
BMI cannot tell muscle from fat, so it overestimates body fat in very muscular people. Treat it as a screening number, not a diagnosis — waist circumference and body-fat percentage add useful context.

🧬 How accurate is the calorie number?
Mifflin-St Jeor predicts BMR within roughly 5–10% for most healthy adults. The activity multiplier is an estimate, so treat the result as a starting point and adjust by real-world weight trends.

🍝 How much weight change is “X” calories?
About 3,500 kcal equals one pound (≈ 7,700 kcal per kg). A daily deficit or surplus of ~500 kcal moves weight by about 1 lb (0.45 kg) per week.

🤷‍♂️ Does this work for athletes or pregnancy?
BMI cutoffs and BMR formulas are less meaningful for athletes and during pregnancy. For medical advice, ask a doctor or dietitian — this tool is for general information only.

🔒 Is my data sent anywhere?
Never. All calculations run in your browser with zero network calls.

📐 Quick formulas
BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²   or   703 × weight (lb) ÷ height (in)²
BMR (men) = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age + 5  ·  BMR (women) = same − 161
Maintenance = BMR × activity: 1.2 sedentary · 1.375 light · 1.55 moderate · 1.725 active · 1.9 very active
~3,500 kcal ≈ 1 lb  ·  ±500 kcal/day ≈ 1 lb/week
