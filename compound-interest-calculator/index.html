💰 Compound Interest Calculator

Savings & investing · Monthly deposits · Year-by-year growth

📘 About this tool

See what your money can become. Start with an initial deposit, add a monthly contribution, pick an expected annual rate, and choose how often interest is compounded — monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or yearly. The calculator projects the future value, separates your own deposits from the interest earned, and shows the balance growing year by year.

Use it to compare a high-yield savings account versus investing, to sanity-check a 401(k) or IRA projection, or just to see how much earlier — or bigger — monthly deposits matter. Everything runs 100% in your browser: no account, no email, no upload.

🧮 What is compound interest?
Interest on interest. Each period your balance earns interest, and next period that interest earns interest too — that is why long-term growth is not a straight line.

💰 How are monthly deposits handled?
Interest is credited at the end of each compounding period at the annual rate divided by the periods per year (monthly = 12, quarterly = 4, semi-annually = 2, yearly = 1), applied to the balance that has been in the account for the whole period. Monthly deposits are added at the end of each month and start earning from the next period.

📈 Is this a guarantee?
No — rates change and investing involves risk. Treat the result as a planning estimate; savings accounts usually pay an APY quoted after compounding, so entering that APY as the rate is a reasonable approximation.

🧾 Does it include taxes or inflation?
No — the projection is in nominal dollars. For an after-tax or "today's money" view, use a lower rate that accounts for taxes and inflation.

🔒 Is my information uploaded?
No — all calculations happen locally on your device, and the offline copy needs no internet.

📐 Key formulas
Lump-sum compound: A = P × (1 + r/f)^(f × t) — P = principal, r = annual rate, f = compounding periods per year, t = years
Simple interest: A = P × (1 + r × t)
This tool adds monthly deposits month by month: at each credit date, interest applies to the balance held for the full period, then that month's deposit is added
