📝 Resume & Cover Letter Builder

ATS-friendly one-page resume · matching cover letter · print or save as PDF · private & offline

📘 About this tool

Applying for jobs in the US usually means two documents: a resume an applicant tracking system (ATS) can read, and a cover letter that shows why you fit. This builder keeps both on one page. Fill in contact details, a summary, reverse-chronological experience with measurable bullets, education, and skills — the resume preview stays single-column with standard headings (Experience, Education, Skills), exactly the plain structure ATS parsers expect. The cover letter tab turns the same details into a short, targeted letter you can tailor for each job.

Everything is typed and printed locally: no account, no upload, no AI scanning your data. Use your browser's print dialog to export a clean PDF, then submit that file with your application.

🤖 What does "ATS-friendly" mean?
Applicant tracking systems scan resumes for text and standard headings. Avoid photos, tables, columns, and graphics — plain single-column text with clear section titles parses most reliably.

📄 How long should my resume be?
One page for most early and mid-career applicants; two pages only when you have 10+ years of directly relevant experience. Aim for roughly 400–600 words.

📈 How do I write strong bullets?
Start with an action verb and quantify results when possible ("Cut page load time by 40%", "Led a team of 5"). Numbers stand out to both parsers and recruiters.

✉️ How long should a cover letter be?
Three to four short paragraphs (under one page). Mention the specific role and company, give two or three concrete strengths, and end with a clear next step.

🔒 Is my data uploaded?
No. Everything is generated in your browser and never leaves your device.

📷 Should I add a photo?
For most US jobs, no — ATS software and recruiters prefer text-only resumes, and photos can raise bias concerns. Add one only when the job ad, industry (acting, modeling, media), or the country's convention expects it. This builder keeps it optional, top-right in the header if used.

📐 Quick formulas
Standard section order: Contact → Summary → Experience → Education → Skills.
Bullet formula: action verb + task + result with a number.
One resume page ≈ 400–600 words; cover letter ≤ 1 page, 3–4 paragraphs.
File type for ATS: PDF (selectable text) or .docx — never a photo or scanned image.
