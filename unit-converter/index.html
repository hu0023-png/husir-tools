🧮 Unit Converter

Length · Weight · Temperature · Volume · Speed · Data — US & metric

📘 About this tool

Convert between US customary and metric units in seconds — inches to centimeters, pounds to kilograms, Fahrenheit to Celsius, cups to milliliters, miles to kilometers, and more. It is built for everyday American needs: recipes that ask for cups and teaspoons, road trips in miles, package weights in pounds, and weather in Fahrenheit.

The converter runs 100% in your browser with no upload and no account. Choose a category, type a value, and the result updates instantly; the small reference line shows the key conversion factor for the pair you selected.

🧮 Are the conversion factors exact?
Yes — where an exact international definition exists (inch = 2.54 cm, pound = 0.45359237 kg, US gallon = 3.785411784 L), the tool uses it. Results are rounded for display only.

🌡️ How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?
Use (°F − 32) × 5/9. The tool applies the formula automatically when you pick °F and °C.

🥄 Can it help with cooking?
Yes — volume mode includes US teaspoons, tablespoons, fluid ounces, cups, pints, quarts, and gallons alongside milliliters and liters.

💾 Is data storage decimal or binary?
Data mode uses the everyday 1024 convention (1 MB = 1024 KB), matching how operating systems display file sizes.

🔒 Does it need internet?
No — everything is computed locally, and the offline copy works with zero internet.

📐 Key conversion factors
1 in = 2.54 cm (exact) · 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg (exact) · 1 US gal = 3.785411784 L (exact)
°F to °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9 · °C to °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
1 mile = 1.609344 km · 1 cup = 236.5882365 mL · 1 mph = 1.609344 km/h
