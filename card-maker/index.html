🎨 Quote Card & Logo Maker

Social-ready graphics from text · export PNG · runs 100% in your browser

📘 About this tool

Need a clean graphic for an Instagram post, an X announcement, or a course thumbnail — without opening a design app? Quote Card & Logo Maker draws your words onto a canvas at full print-ready resolution and exports a PNG in one click.

Quote cards: pick a preset gradient background, type the quote and attribution, and the text is centered and sized automatically so it always fits. Logo mode: type a name and it generates a two-letter monogram on a colored rounded square or circle — the kind of minimal mark that works as a profile picture, favicon, or placeholder logo.

Because the whole page runs locally, your quotes, brand names, and designs never leave your device, and the page works fully offline. Export sizes match common social formats (1080×1080, 1200×630, 1600×900, 1080×1350) plus 512 and 1024 logo sizes.

📱 What size should I pick?
Instagram feed & LinkedIn = square or 1080×1350 portrait. X and Facebook link cards = 1200×630. Course/slide covers = 1600×900.

🖼️ Where is the PNG saved?
Your browser downloads it to the usual Downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

🎨 Why does my text look small on the preview?
The preview is scaled down to fit the screen; the exported file is full resolution and crisp. Text auto-shrinks to fit the longest line.

🔑 Can I make a logo with more than two letters?
Logo mode takes the initials of the first two words. For a full wordmark, use quote mode and keep the text short.
