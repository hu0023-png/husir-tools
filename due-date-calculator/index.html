🤰 Pregnancy Due Date Calculator

Due date from LMP or conception · Current week & trimester · Full-term window

📘 About this tool

Expecting? Your due date is the anchor for every prenatal appointment and milestone. This calculator estimates it from the date most people know best — the first day of their last menstrual period (LMP) — using Naegele's rule: add 280 days (40 weeks) and adjust for a cycle that is not 28 days. If you know your conception date instead, the tool converts it to an approximate LMP and works from there.

Beyond the due date you also get today's gestational age in weeks and days, the trimester you are in, how far along the pregnancy is, and the realistic full-term window from 38 to 42 weeks — helpful because only about 4–5% of babies arrive exactly on their due date. Early ultrasound dating at 8–13 weeks is usually the most accurate estimate, so treat this as a planning date to confirm with your provider.

Everything is calculated locally in your browser with no account or upload, and no health information is stored or transmitted.

📅 How is the due date calculated?
From LMP: due date = LMP + 280 days + (cycle length − 28). From conception: LMP ≈ conception date − 14 days, then the same rule applies.

🧮 How do I read gestational age?
Pregnancy is counted from LMP, so at week 10 you are actually about 8 weeks past conception. The tool shows "X weeks + Y days" from LMP, which is the convention doctors use.

📆 When is my baby "due"?
Full term is 38–42 weeks. Babies born at 37–38 weeks are early term, 39–40 full term, and 41–42 late term — all within the normal window.

🔬 Is an ultrasound date more accurate?
Usually yes for the first trimester. If your ultrasound due date differs by more than a week, providers typically switch to the scan date.

⚠️ Is this medical advice?
No. This is a date-estimation tool only. Share the result with your OB/GYN or midwife, who will confirm it at your first visit.

📐 Quick formulas
Due date = LMP + 280 days + (cycle length − 28)
Conception ≈ LMP + 14 days
Full-term window = 38 to 42 weeks from LMP
Trimester 1: 0–13 wks · Trimester 2: 14–27 wks · Trimester 3: 28 wks+
