🧦 Clothing & Shoe Size Converter

US ↔ EU ↔ UK · women · men · kids · runs 100% in your browser

📘 About this tool

Shopping across countries means decoding three different size systems on one label. This converter translates US sizes into EU, UK, and centimeter equivalents for the cases that matter most: women's and men's shoes, kids' shoes, women's dress sizes, men's suit jackets, and men's shirt collars.

Pick a category, type a US size, and the matching sizes update instantly. Every conversion uses the standard industry chart relationships, so you can compare a US 8 women's shoe to EU, UK, and foot length without opening five browser tabs. Sizes are marked approximate on purpose — brands cut differently, and the only truly reliable method is measuring your foot in centimeters or checking the brand's own chart.

Everything runs locally on your device: no account, no upload, no tracking, fully offline. Keep it open next to your shopping cart.

🧦 What is my US size if I only know my foot in cm?
As a rough guide, women's foot length in cm minus 17 gives a US size (25 cm ≈ US 8), and men's cm minus 18 gives a US size (28 cm ≈ US 10). Half sizes sit between.

🏷️ Are women's US and EU sizes really just +30?
Roughly. Most charts put EU ≈ US + 30 for women's shoes (US 7 ≈ EU 37). Brands can differ by a full size, especially with heels and running shoes.

👦 Why are kids' shoe sizes so confusing?
Kid sizes restart after US 13: toddler and little-kid sizes run about EU +16–17, then “big kid” US 1–7 maps to EU 32 and up. Age is a terrible guide — use the foot.

👕 What does EU 38 mean on a men's shirt?
It is the collar in centimeters (15-inch collar ≈ EU 38 cm). Suit jackets are different: US 40 ≈ EU 50, roughly US + 10.

🔍 Is this exact?
No converter can be. Use it to shortlist sizes, then confirm with the brand's chart or a tape measure before checkout.

📐 Quick reference (approx.)
Shoes — women: EU = US + 30, UK = US − 2.5, cm ≈ US + 17  ·  men: EU = US + 33, UK = US − 1, cm ≈ US + 18
Clothing — women: UK = US + 4, EU = US + 32  ·  men's suit: EU = US + 10  ·  shirt collar EU(cm) = inches × 2.54
