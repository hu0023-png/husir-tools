🔤 Text Case & Word Counter

Title / Sentence / camelCase · Word count + reading time

📘 About this tool

Fix the case of any text in one click — UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case (AP-style headline rules), sentence case, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, and kebab-case — while live counters show characters, words, sentences, lines, and estimated reading time. Ideal for email subject lines, blog headlines, social posts, code identifiers, and proofreading your own writing.

The converter runs entirely in your browser: paste sensitive drafts, cover letters, or notes and nothing is ever uploaded. The counters update as you type, and one click copies the result back to your clipboard.

📰 How does Title Case work here?
It follows the common US headline style: capitalize the first and last words plus every major word, and keep short articles, conjunctions, and prepositions (a, an, the, and, or, for, in, on, of, to, with…) lowercase in the middle.

⏱️ How is reading time estimated?
At the average adult reading speed of about 200 words per minute; speaking time uses about 130 words per minute.

💻 Why would I use camelCase or snake_case?
They are naming conventions for code and data — camelCase for JavaScript-style variables, snake_case for file names and databases, kebab-case for URLs and CSS classes.

🔒 Is my text uploaded?
No — every transformation and count happens locally on your device, and the offline copy needs no internet.

📐 Key formulas
Reading time = word count ÷ 200 words per minute
Speaking time = word count ÷ 130 words per minute
Characters count includes spaces unless noted; word count splits on whitespace
