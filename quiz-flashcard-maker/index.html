🎴 Quiz & Flashcard Maker

Build a deck in minutes · flip & study · self-test quiz · export & share JSON

📘 About this tool

Turn vocabulary words, state capitals, math facts, science terms, or any subject into flashcards and a self-test quiz in a few minutes. Type cards one by one or paste a whole list in "front | back" format — perfect when you already have a study sheet. Practice in two ways: Flashcards let you flip each card and sort out the ones you still need to learn; Quiz shuffles the deck and scores you as you self-check each answer.

The deck is saved automatically in your browser and can be exported as a small JSON file, so students can practice on another device, or teachers can share one deck with the whole class. Everything runs locally — no account, no upload, works offline.

🎯 What is the difference between Flashcards and Quiz?
Flashcards are for learning: you flip cards and mark which ones need more review. Quiz is for testing: you see the question, check the answer, and mark yourself right or wrong to get a score.

⚡ What is the fastest way to make a deck?
Paste one card per line using the format question | answer (or a tab). Example: Sacramento | capital of California. Click "Paste list" and add them all at once.

🔀 Are quiz questions shuffled?
Yes — every quiz starts with a fresh random order, so repeating a deck still challenges your memory.

💾 Will my deck disappear when I close the tab?
No — the current deck is saved in your browser's local storage. Export the JSON file whenever you want a permanent backup or to share it.

🔒 Is anything uploaded?
No. Decks, scores, and your practice stay entirely on your device.

📐 Quick reference
Paste format: one card per line — front | back (pipe or Tab).
Export: deck.json = { "title": "...", "cards": [ {"f": "...", "b": "..."} ] }.
Study tip: review missed cards again after 1 day, 3 days, and 7 days for stronger recall.
