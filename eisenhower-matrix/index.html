📌 Eisenhower Matrix

Sort tasks by urgent & important · saved in your browser · runs 100% locally

📘 About this tool

When everything feels urgent, nothing is. The Eisenhower Matrix (also called the urgent-important matrix) forces a quick two-question decision on every task: Is it urgent — does it need attention right now? Is it important — does it matter for your goals in the long run? The answers drop the task into one of four boxes: Do now, Schedule, Delegate, or Eliminate.

Type a task, pick a starting box, and drag nothing — this tool keeps it simpler than that. Tick tasks off as you finish them, move a task to a different box with one dropdown when its priority changes, and clear completed items when the board gets noisy. Everything is saved automatically in this browser, so a refresh won't lose your plan.

It is built for the way ADHD brains actually work: fewer decisions, visible consequences, and no feature sprawl. Runs 100% on your device — no account, no upload, fully offline.

💡 What goes in each box?
Do now — urgent and important (a deadline today). Schedule — important but not urgent (deep work, planning, exercise). Delegate or minimize — urgent but not important (most emails, interruptions). Eliminate — neither (mindless scrolling, busywork).

🔁 What if a task changes priority?
Open the dropdown on the task and move it. Its position in the matrix updates immediately and is saved.

📋 Where is my data stored?
Only in this browser's local storage on your device. Clearing site data removes it; no server ever sees it.

⏳ I keep procrastinating on Q2 tasks — any trick?
That is normal. Schedule Q2 tasks with a specific time and block out distractions; the matrix's whole point is to make you protect that time before urgency steals it.

🎯 Real-life examples — an ordinary workday
⚡ Do now: finish the report due at 5 PM · take a sick child to urgent care · answer the client who needs it today
📅 Schedule: prep next week’s quarterly review · workout 3× this week · start the online course · book the dentist
🤝 Delegate / minimize: forward the email chain someone else owns · book the conference room · file the low-priority form
🗑️ Eliminate: doomscrolling · meetings with no agenda · rewriting emails that are fine as-is
Same task can move boxes: a “Schedule” task becomes “Do now” the moment its deadline arrives — use the dropdown on the task to move it.
📐 Decision rule
Urgent + important = Do now  ·  Important, not urgent = Schedule  ·  Urgent, not important = Delegate / minimize  ·  Neither = Eliminate
