💰 Monthly Budget Tracker

Income · Fixed & variable spending · What is left to save

📘 About this tool

See where a month of money really goes. Enter your monthly take-home income, list fixed bills (rent, utilities, subscriptions), then estimate variable spending (groceries, dining, gas, fun). The tracker totals each group and shows what is left to save — the number that actually matters each month.

Budgets are saved only in this browser for the month you pick, so you can compare months side by side or start fresh. Nothing is uploaded, no bank connection, no ads — just your numbers on your device.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Estimates for planning only — not tax or financial advice. Rates and rules change, so verify with your state or a qualified professional before filing or making big decisions.

🎯 What is the 50/30/20 rule?
A popular US guideline: 50% of after-tax income for needs, 30% for wants, 20% for savings/debt. Use the example button to see how it looks, then adjust to your real numbers.

📦 What counts as fixed vs variable?
Fixed bills are roughly the same every month (rent, internet, insurance). Variable spending changes with choices (groceries, eating out, shopping) — so it is the lever you can pull to free up cash.

🔁 Is my budget saved online?
No — it is stored in your browser's local storage on this device only. Clear the month or your browser data to reset.

💾 Does it work offline?
Yes — everything runs locally with no internet needed.

📐 Key formulas
Remaining = income − fixed expenses − variable expenses
Savings rate = remaining ÷ income × 100%
50/30/20 split: needs 50% · wants 30% · savings 20% of after-tax income
