📅 Attendance Tracker

Click a cell to cycle: P present · L late · A absent · E excused

📘 About this tool

Keep a lightweight daily attendance log for one class without a grade book, app, or account. Paste your roster once, tap "＋ Add today" each school day, and click through the roll: P present, L late, A absent, E excused. Each column shows the day's totals, each row shows the student's running record — days attended, times late, absences — and the percent of days attended is calculated for you.

This is the kind of record homeroom teachers, club sponsors, and tutors need for parent conferences, report-card comments, or referral paperwork. When the period ends, export the whole grid as CSV to open in Excel or Google Sheets, or print the sheet for a sub folder or a paper file. The class saves automatically in your browser, so you can reopen it tomorrow and keep going. Everything is local: no upload, no login, and the offline copy works on a school Chromebook with no internet.

🔁 How do I mark a student?
Click a cell to cycle through P → L → A → E → blank. Use the legend colors to scan the day at a glance; column footers show counts like "P 22 · L 1 · A 1".

📊 How is "attended" counted?
Attended = Present + Late (the student was in school). Attendance % = attended ÷ total days marked for that student. Excused and unexcused absences both lower it, but only unexcused count as "Absent" in the row summary.

📆 Do I have to add every date by hand?
No — "＋ Add today" appends the current date once per day. Use "Add another date…" to backfill or pre-add future days (e.g., the whole week).

💾 Where is my data saved?
In your browser's local storage on this device. Export CSV whenever you want a file to keep, email, or put in your LMS.

🔒 Is this private?
Yes. Student names and attendance never leave your computer.

📐 Quick reference
Attendance % = (P + L) ÷ (P + L + A + E) × 100 per student, using only marked days.
Row summary: P = present · L = late · A = absent (unexcused) · E = excused.
Tip: click the small × on a date header to remove a day (e.g., a mis-added date).
